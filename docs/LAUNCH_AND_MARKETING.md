# Low-cost launch and 50,000-view plan

Goal: at least **50,000 cumulative page views before Worlds begins October 15, 2026**, with no paid ads and no uncapped traffic cost.

## Measurement

Use Vercel page views as the headline measure and these product events as quality signals: champion selected, Build A set, result shared, champion-pool add, feedback useful, and wrong-number report. Review weekly; do not add another analytics product unless the current data cannot answer a launch decision.

Milestones:

| Date | Cumulative views | Weekly focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Aug 16 | 2,500 | private testers, launch assets, patch 26.16 |
| Aug 31 | 7,500 | first repeatable patch-day format |
| Sep 15 | 17,500 | champion/build SEO pages and creator outreach |
| Sep 30 | 32,500 | Worlds pool content and share-card loop |
| Oct 14 | 50,000 | daily Worlds countdown and final patch coverage |

This requires roughly 700 views per day on average. Patch days should carry several thousand views so ordinary days can be lower.

## Positioning

One sentence: **“RiftIntel shows what a League patch does to your champion pool and actual spell rotation—not just the raw notes.”**

Lead with one concrete result per post: “Patch 26.16 changes this combo by X damage against a 100-MR target.” Do not lead with a generic product announcement.

## Channel plan at $0

1. **Search:** publish the verified patch page immediately, keep item/champion URLs in the sitemap, and add one focused explanation for the five most searched changed champions.
2. **Reddit:** contribute a complete useful summary in communities that permit tools/self-promotion; disclose ownership and follow each community's rules. One strong patch thread beats repeated links.
3. **Short video:** make 20–40 second clips showing the raw note, calculator rotation, and result card. Reuse vertically on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and X.
4. **X/Bluesky:** post one overview graphic plus separate champion replies. Link to the exact champion/calculator state, not only the homepage.
5. **Discord:** ask moderators before posting. Offer a clean patch summary their members can use without requiring promotion placement.
6. **Creators/coaches:** send 10 personalized messages per patch to small/mid-sized champion specialists. Give them a ready-made calculator link/image and ask for an accuracy check, not a paid shoutout.
7. **Owned return loop:** ask users to save a champion pool and subscribe to `/feed.xml`. No email platform is needed initially.

## Weekly operating cadence

- Monday: prepare likely changed champions and reusable video/card templates.
- Patch-notes day: validate data, publish the patch overview, five champion examples, and one short video.
- Day +1: publish Build A/B or combo comparisons for the top two changes.
- Day +2: answer questions and publish corrections/clarifications.
- Weekend: one evergreen champion-history or “best way to test a build” guide.
- Sunday: review traffic sources, useful-rate, share-rate, and returning usage; keep only formats that worked.

Use Riot's official schedule as the source of truth. Current pre-Worlds opportunities are scheduled around Aug 12, Aug 26, Sep 9, Sep 23, and Oct 7; reconfirm every date on Riot Support before preparing timed posts.

## Launch content pack

- 60-second product walkthrough: Champion Pool → changed champion → combo builder → shared result.
- Three result cards using popular changed champions.
- One honest accuracy post explaining verified versus archive-review data.
- One “how damage is modeled” page/video showing target armor/MR and confidence labels.
- Worlds countdown post linking to `/worlds`.

## Conversion targets

- At least 8% of calculator visitors select a non-default champion.
- At least 3% set Build A.
- At least 2% share a link or result image.
- At least 5% of homepage visitors add a champion to their pool.
- At least 80% useful responses on verified current-patch pages.

If reach is high but these are low, fix the product/onboarding before increasing promotion.

## Revenue sequence

1. **Now:** free noncommercial validation; no ads or payments.
2. **After Riot status + commercial hosting:** test one flat-price, manually sold sponsor placement for a month, clearly labeled and policy-compatible. A fixed agreement avoids traffic-meter risk.
3. **After repeat usage is proven:** offer optional paid workflow value—cross-device workspaces, private notes, export packs, or team boards—while keeping core patch facts/calculator free.
4. **Only after meaningful traffic:** consider lightweight contextual ads with a monthly revenue/cost review. Avoid ad-tech that introduces excessive tracking.

Do not build accounts, a database, or payment flows until local champion pools and sharing demonstrate repeat use. Do not use paid acquisition before organic content converts.

## Stop rules

- Pause a post if the patch is quarantined or the official notes are still changing.
- Stop a channel after three well-executed posts with no qualified visits.
- Do not spend money to rescue a weak activation rate.
- Pause all revenue work if Riot status or hosting terms are unclear.
